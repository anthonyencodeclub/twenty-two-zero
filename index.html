How to play

The idea

Draw squads from the Women's Super League's whole history — Arsenal 2011 to the present day — and draft a starting XI, one player at a time.

Then you play a full 22-game WSL season: eleven rivals, home and away, a live table, a title race and a run-in.

22 wins, 0 defeats is the perfect season. Nobody has done it.

Drafting

Every draw lands on one club-season and its squad opens. Pick a player, then choose her shirt — a slot in the XI or a place on the bench. Out of position her rating drops; a full-back at centre-back is fine, a striker in goal is not.

You draft 20 players: a starting XI plus a bench of 1 GK, 3 defenders, 2 midfielders and 3 attackers. Rotation happens — a deep bench keeps the season alive.

Then name a captain. Leadership is hidden in every player: the right armband rallies comebacks at half-time.

Tactics

Set your approach between matches and change it at half-time: all-out attack, balanced, or park the bus. Chasing a title decider is different from protecting a lead.

Chemistry rewards real football: players who actually played together, clubmates across eras, countrymates, and anyone in their proper position.

Scoring

Win 60 · Draw 25 · goals +3 / −2 · Champions +250 · Unbeaten +150 · Perfect 22-0 +400. Modes multiply it. The server recomputes every run.
